# Deepmarks Android 2.2.8

Share-to-save reliability and faster autofill.

- **Share without opening the app.** Saving a link from the system share
  sheet now signs and posts it to Deepmarks in the background, so the
  bookmark appears on the web and your other devices without you having
  to open the app afterward. (Applies to local-key sign-ins — nsec,
  passkey, or recovery key. Remote-signer sign-ins still open the app to
  finish, since the key isn't on the device.)
- **Faster metadata autofill.** The share sheet and the in-app "add a
  bookmark" form now populate title, description, and suggested tags as
  fast as possible; richer AI tags are still added to the saved bookmark
  in the background.
