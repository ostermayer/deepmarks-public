# Deepmarks Android 1.7

- Adds the Friends tab for selected NIP-02 contacts, including explicit Deepmarks bookmarks and links friends posted in kind:1 Nostr notes.
- Adds efficient rich previews for friends links: direct image previews, click-to-play audio/video, YouTube thumbnails, and cached webpage snippets.
- Keeps friends links shareable from the feed even before you save them, and zaps the friend or falls back through the normal Deepmarks zap path.
- Keeps RSS/Atom feed icons web-only; native mobile apps do not show feed-subscription controls.
- Includes synchronized web/mobile bundle fixes for friends search, sorting, and feed actions.
