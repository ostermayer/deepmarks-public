# Deepmarks 0.8.1

Deepmarks 0.8.1 is a **mobile share + archive-reliability** release. The
biggest change is that saving a link from your phone's share sheet now
reaches Deepmarks on its own, and tweet/media archiving got more
dependable. This is a server/source release — nothing about the user's
data model, keys, or portability changes. If Deepmarks vanished, every
bookmark you saved is still readable from Nostr, exactly as before.

## Share to save — without opening the app

Saving a link from the iOS or Android system share sheet now **signs and
posts the bookmark in the background**, so it reaches the relays and shows
up on the web and your other devices without you having to open the app
afterward.

- This applies to local-key sign-ins (nsec, passkey, recovery key).
  Remote-signer sign-ins (NIP-46 / bunker / Android NIP-55) still open the
  app to finish, since the signing key isn't on the device.
- On iOS this previously fell back to opening the app every time: the
  share extension reads its own copy of the key from a shared Keychain
  item, which native sign-in never populated. The app now mirrors the key
  there on login and on relaunch, and clears it on logout.

See [`mobile.md`](../mobile.md) for the full per-platform model.

## Faster metadata autofill

The share sheet and the in-app "add a bookmark" form now populate title,
description, and suggested tags **as fast as possible**: they request the
`/metadata` endpoint with `enrich=0`, which skips the server's blocking
inline LLM call. The saved bookmark is still enriched with AI summaries
and tags by the backend backfill once it publishes, and page metadata
stays Redis-cached. See [`llm-augmentation.md`](../llm-augmentation.md).

## Archiving reliability

- **Tweet rescues are rebuilt through FixTweet** against canonical
  `x.com`, replacing the previous Nitter-style mirror path — those public
  mirrors had gone dark and were removed entirely.
- **Smarter media-archive retries.** Recoverable media-archive failures
  retry quickly; permanently-gone media is rechecked on a slower monthly
  cadence instead of being abandoned.
- **YouTube + video hardening.** Tweet video is capped at 720p like the
  rest of the pipeline, and YouTube capture uses cookies as an auth-gate
  fallback rather than globally. An `archive-retry` admin command re-runs
  recoverable failures.

Detail: [`archives.md`](../archives.md).
